# WORLD X HEROES Art Bible

## North Star

The human is the character. Each Hero and Familiar belongs to one connected universe, while their culture, genre, silhouette, environment, and materials respond to the individual rather than a fixed fantasy stereotype. The provided World X Heroes gateway and character references are the primary visual benchmark for scale, detail, atmosphere, and finish.

## Image Language

- **Lighting:** cinematic motivated light, readable faces, atmospheric rays, rich shadow detail, and controlled highlights. Let the environment explain the light source.
- **Color:** pearl and cloud silver, antique gold, deep ink, and restrained crimson are shared anchors. Each world can introduce its own palette; avoid one-hue treatments.
- **Characters:** recognizable human identity when an approved portrait is supplied; natural anatomy, expressive faces, distinctive hair, detailed clothing, accessories, and believable materials.
- **Creatures:** species-specific anatomy and personality. A Familiar may be animal, spirit, mythical, aquatic, mechanical, or original; never default every player to the same creature.
- **Worlds:** layered foreground, subject, and distant environment; large-scale architecture, terrain, weather, civilizations, and small narrative details.
- **Fantasy / science fiction:** blend according to the Hero's approved interests, skills, and preferences. Do not force a culture, era, class, or aesthetic.
- **Materials:** distinguish fabric, metal, stone, fur, feathers, scales, glass, and energy through texture, wear, reflectance, and light response.
- **Composition:** one clear focal subject, dramatic but legible pose, environmental context, deliberate negative space for interface overlays, and a shared camera perspective for Hero and Familiar.
- **Atmosphere:** use scene-relevant clouds, mist, rain, snow, particles, water, embers, or starlight sparingly. Motion is subtle and must respect reduced-motion settings.
- **Progression:** preserve the Hero's recognizable identity. Earned milestones may evolve silhouette, equipment, emblem, environment, lighting, pose, companion, and scale.

## Screen Environments

- **Gateway:** a vast threshold into multiple civilizations and floating worlds; the title and entry action remain legible over the scene.
- **Creation:** a threshold/chamber where the player's story is heard and a distinct Familiar arrives.
- **Hero domain:** a personal environment shaped by approved identity, documented skills, chosen preferences, and earned accomplishments.
- **Familiar:** a habitat and character portrait with enough scale to read personality and anatomy.
- **Progression / archive:** monumental halls, galleries, and artifacts that explain verified growth rather than popularity.
- **Future modules:** exploration, connection, and challenges inherit the same lighting, material, and typography rules while receiving their own environment and palette.

## Interface Integration

Artwork is the environment, not a wallpaper tile. Place controls in restrained translucent panels with readable contrast, fine engraved borders, and minimal gold light accents. Keep inputs and progression explanations clear. Do not cover focal faces with controls; do not use decorative effects as substitutes for artwork.

## Generation Contract

A generation request must be individualized using only player-approved inputs. Attach a portrait only after explicit consent. By default, do not include private Familiar conversation memory, sealed evidence, or unapproved personal details. Preserve an art version and evolution stage with generated outputs so rank-up artwork can build on the same Hero identity. A provider must be configured before any request is transmitted; frontend prompt construction alone is not image generation.
